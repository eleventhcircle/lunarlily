Nonfiction I'm reading

Current theme is studying the art of storycraft. I've got a couple big Projects™ I'm too scared to start, and I think it's in part because I feel like such an inadqeuate storyteller. It's not like I'm gonna improve by just sitting on my ass doing nothing, so I'm picking through a few different books to find nuggets of wisdom on the topics where I really struggle.

Bird by Bird: I had some high school friends who took a creative writing course for one of their credits (I remember being jealous at the time, I didn't have room in my schedule for it) and they read through some or all of this book as part of the classwork, I'm not too sure if it was the whole thing or just excerpts. It struck a chord with them and I trusted their taste in books, so of course I filed it away as a book to check out... and then promptly forgot about it for ten years. So these things go.

Written in a very approachable and honestly really funny way, but Lamott has a knack for cutting right to the deepest parts of you. The chapter on perfectionism spoke to something so fundamental to my being that all I could do for a while was stare at the wall in silence and sit with what I had read.

Manga in Theory and Practice: This is the part where I must shamefully admit that I've never read any of Jojo's Bizarre Adventures. I've seen and heard enough good about it to give this book a whirl, though, and I'm liking it so far! Since one of those big Projects™ I've been avoiding is intended to be in a comic format, it felt prudent to do some studying on visual storytelling and related topics.

(If it's technically a week overdue back to the library right now... well. I trust you not to tattle. I'm working on it I promise.)

Semi-related: I'm kinda getting into A5 size notebooks and binders this year...?! I have a refillable one for notetaking about storycraft, a spiral from Miniso that I use as a catch-all notebook, a couple cute clear binders for solo journaling games... I even turned one into a sticker book! It's the perfect size for the Stickii Club packs I've collected over the years.

A5 is such a nice size for writing: not so big that it's tiring to handwrite, not so small that I feel like I'm fighting to cram all my thoughts onto the page. I've been out of academic settings long enough that I've lost all the stamina I built up, my hand cramps really easily now lol. I just need to figure out setting up my printer for A5 paper and then I'll be golden.

This year's planner is the same as last year, a weekly Leuchtturm1917 (again in A5 lol), but for the first time in many years I'm not bothering to decorate each spread. It still works just as well without the washi tape and the stickers, of course, though it's much less whimsical... maybe next year I'll have the energy to decorate again.

I mentioned this in a Thing of the Week somewhat recently I think? Or maybe it was in a journal, I don't remember for sure. But I've been getting into tabbing up my books in a big way lately. Very early on in my experimenting, I discovered a preference for plastic tabs over paper (less prone to tearing/folding/otherwise getting damaged on the shelf) which was a bit of a problem; all of my index tabs leftover from college are paper. Not to worry, though: these little index tabs from Daiso are doing all of the heavy lifting for me now. They're sooooo cute aren't they?

Games I'm playing (as spoiler-free as possible!)

My friend Byleth recently told me about his approach to his backlog and defeating that sense of choice paralysis when there's a hundred different games you could be playing. He limits himself to three games in active rotation, and makes a separate list of a dozen games he considers "up next" in the proverbial queue. When he sits down to play something, he's only got three games to deliberate between. When he finishes one of those three, he only has to consider what to play next from a small list he already curated to his current interests. It's so straightforward when you write it out like that but idk it kind of turned my brain upside down? So I'm giving it a try myself. Maybe I'll actually start making a dent in my "play this soon" list lol

Fields of Mistria: 1.0 released at the beginning of August and it has taken one group of my friends by storm lol. I'm having a great time (slowly) making my way through the story, of course, but I'm having just as much fun talking with them about their farmers, answering obscure questions about game systems, and making big wet eyes at all the beautiful fanart together. An unexpected but very welcome source of bonding between us this year (and it inspires much less interpersonal violence than Super Battle Golf).

I'm playing an unmodded save right now; I just broke the second-to-last seal in the mines a couple play sessions ago and I got to 8 hearts with a townie for the first time! (it was Eiland) (no one was surprised by this)

I spent three IRL hours decorating my house after getting the first upgrade... very proud of it, very not looking forward to getting more house to decorate later

Fire Emblem Fortune's Weave: I really thought this would be a skip for me; I'm not much of a Fire Emblem guy (baby's first was Awakening and it's still the only one I've ever finished) and I really wasn't cheesed about the price tag at launch... I got a copy for my partner as a gift and figured I could just borrow it in a year or two if I got the itch.

Alas. It took two days of me watching him play before I fully caved and bought myself a copy. I sweat they put some kind of secret sauce in this game that's making me go bonkers, I don't know how to explain it but it's scratching an itch I didn't even know was itchy. He's playing Dietrich's route first; I went with Cai, and I just made it to his chapter 2 today. There's so much shit to do in this game, I suspect it'll be in rotation for a long while...

He hit the gomen oomfchan

Final Fantasy XIV: OK to be fair—when am I not playing XIV? It's almost always there in the background, the home I can always come back to. It feels worthy of mention right now because of the recent Switch 2 version release. The ability to log in anywhere has lowkey revolutionized my world; no longer is access to my comfort game locked behind whether I can tolerate sitting at my desk for more than a few minutes! Going without mods is a bit of a bummer but not a deal-breaker for me, since I'm not playing on the Switch with the intent of gposing or anything. This thing is my chip-away-at-long-term-grinds-in-bed machine, and I don't need my WOLs to look perfect for that.

Character House Desktop Companion (demo): Last minute bonus entry. Calling this one a game is kind of misleading but I dunno what else it could be categorized as. Focus helper...? Parallel play buddy? You get a little guy (or several little guys!) you can dress up and interact with. It reminds me of shimejis but with much more customization potential. I like it a lot so far!

Shows I'm watching

Witch Hat Atelier: To be honest, I was watching weekly but paused at episode 10... I don't want it to be over, so I don't want to catch up lol. But I must, because if I don't finish it then my partner won't finish it, and I can't allow that.

I've been following the manga for a long time so I knew I would like the anime just fine, but my goodness... seeing Shirahama's work come to life is magical. Hearing it, too—I really love the OST, and all the little sounds that come together to make the world feel so lush and textured... ough.

Secrets of the Silent Witch: Now that I'm past volume 4 of the novels, I'm safe to watch the anime without any spoilers! Verdict: I don't like it nearly as much as the novels LOL

My favorite part of this series has been watching Monica's slow growth and connection to the people around her, but by its very nature the anime can't take its time in the same way the novel can... the relationships all feel incredibly flat to me because of the pacing. Visually it's lovely and I've snorted at seeing some of the goofs animated but unfortunately I am one of those "read the book it's better" people on this one. Hell, even the manga has better pacing (though I still think not really getting time in each character's head is to the detriment of any adaptation).

Romeo x Juliet: Picking this one up on request from a friend! I'm meant to be doing a group watch with some pals but I missed the first day because I wasn't feeling good, I ended up napping most of that day away. So I need to get caught up before we keep going.

I'd never heard of this series before my friend brought it up, so I'm not going into it with much in the way of expectations. I just want to enjoy seeing something new and hanging out with pals :]

Light novels I'm reading

Secrets of the Silent Witch: Monica Everett, my beloved!! I've got three unread volumes left on my shelf right now; the eighth came out a couple weeks ago and it's just as gorgeous as its companions. I really don't know what I'm going to do with myself after that... the eternal wait for volume 9 (sobbing). I think they're up to 13 or 14 in Japanese right now, serializing the Gaiden arc. I know there's some fan translations floating around out there, in case I get impatient waiting for the official releases...

7th Time Loop: Sleeper hit series! I didn't think I'd like it as much as I do. It's got me curious to try some other titles in the "villainess" subgenre... I must admit: I read Riche and Arnold as 5-6 years older than they actually are in canon, though. They both read too mature for me to buy them being teenagers LOL

Tracking these down in print has been another nightmare scenario like with Silent Witch. I ended up putting in requests for Interlibrary loans for volumes 4-6 so I wasn't stuck waiting for reprints to get caught up (many thanks to the Pima County library system for coming in clutch), and I've been scavenging secondhand booksellers for months to fill out my personal collection. I'm very happy to say that I just got the last volume I was missing in the mail yesterday...!
