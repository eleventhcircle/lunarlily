Thing of the Week

Week of August 16, 2026: flatmode gaming

Posted August 21

Been getting back into handheld gaming in bed in a big way lately. Not that I ever really fell out of it, per se, but these sorts of things come in waves, y'know? This summer delivered unto me several games I've been eagerly anticipating: Splatoon Raiders, Fields of Mistria 1.0, FFXIV on the Switch 2, and most recently Over Requiemz; to say I'm eating good would be an understatement.

I've been on-again-off-again with my Steam Deck for roundabouts a year, now; my model is an old one from 2022, or maybe early 2023? With only 256GB of storage onboard. At the time I thought surely that would be enough, I didn't need to shell out the extra dosh for a bigger drive... but alas. Video games are big! Storage micromanagement and inconsistent performance got on my nerves enough that I eventually tucked the Deck away entirely for a while. Having an entire ass console that I wasn't using at all made me feel kinda dumb, though; in July I finally caved and picked up a 1TB SSD, rated with much better read speeds than the stock drive. I even got 80% of the way through installing it before a stripped screw on the battery shield got the best of me. Sometime in the next couple weeks I'll bring it to a local repair shop and see if they can help me finish the installation. In the meantime, it is exclusively my farming-in-bed machine.
